# Gabriel setup and delivery plan

Goal: the gift opens in Gabriel's own Claude, on his computer and phone, with as few steps as possible.

## What Gabriel needs (checklist for the card)
1. Accept the invitation to the page (email from Claude/Delsy, Editor access). Needed so his Mission Log can be saved privately.
2. Open the gift link while signed in to claude.ai.
3. Allow Notion for the page when Claude asks. Notion must be connected in claude.ai > Settings > Connectors, with access to the "Brief Diario de Trabajo" page.
4. Pin the page (so it sits in his sidebar) and add the link to his phone home screen.
5. Optional, later: connect Google Calendar and Outlook (same Connectors screen) so "Today's Meetings" fills in.

## Open decisions
- Morning delivery: link + pin (always works) vs a scheduled task in his Claude vs an email digest to himself. Test in his account before promising any of them.
- Card language (English or Spanish).
- Card link: the final shared link of the page.
- Whether Gabriel as Editor may change the page. Editor can republish it. Contributor cannot write his private log if he is outside the organization.
